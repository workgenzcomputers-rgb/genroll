How "unlimited" works here — in plain terms

Unlimited refers to access, not usage. Every plan can open every tool and select every model listed in the catalogue. There is no Pro-only model, no locked resolution and no feature paywall between tiers.

What the plan price buys is a shared monthly allowance pool, quoted in rupees. Each generation draws down that pool at the underlying provider's metered rate — a $0.022/sec video model and a $0.247/sec video model consume the same pool at very different speeds. When the pool is spent, generation pauses until you top up or the next cycle begins. This is not true unlimited usage, and we will not describe it as such.

Rates shown throughout Genroll are the provider's published list rates at the time of sourcing, converted for display. They can move without notice when a provider repricing lands, and per-request rounding rules differ by vendor. Treat every volume estimate on this page as an estimate. Unused allowance does not carry over.
